1. **The big idea**
The 4D Framework (Delegation, Description, Discernment, Diligence) teaches how I collaborate with AI.
This course teaches how AI is able to work with me.
Together they form one system: human competencies on one side, machine properties on the other.

 # Analogy (my own): learning to drive. The 4Ds are my driving skills (steering, judging, staying safe). This course is learning how the car actually works (engine, brakes, limits). A good driver needs both.
